Questions

Who is ishéli for?

For women who’ve spent years meeting everyone else’s expectations and want to feel like themselves again — especially if most self-improvement apps leave you feeling behind.

What does it cost?

Chapter 1 is free. Membership is €49.99 a year or €9.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial for new members. Founding members get their first year free (100 places) or at €24.99 (1,000 places), with a reminder 7 days before it ends. Prices may vary by country.

How much time does it take?

About ten minutes, every other day. The Breathe room and small guides are there whenever you need a minute in between.

Is this therapy?

No. ishéli is a companion for reflection and small changes, not medical or psychological care. If you’re struggling, please reach out to a professional — and in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What happens to what I write?

It stays on your iPhone. There’s no account and no server that reads it. Lock it with Face ID, export it, or delete everything any time.

When can I download it? Android?

Soon, on iPhone first. Join the waitlist and you’ll hear first, with your founding codes inside. Android comes later — join now and you’ll get an email the day it’s ready.
