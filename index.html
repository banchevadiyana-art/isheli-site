Subscription details: free months, renewal and cancelling

On launch day you redeem your founding code in the App Store and choose yearly (€29.99) or monthly (€4.99). The first 3 months are free. After that, your subscription renews automatically at the price you chose, charged to your Apple Account, until you cancel. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the free months end in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you allow notifications, the app also reminds you a week before. After launch, without a founding code, ishéli is €39.99 a year or €6.99 a month, and Chapter 1 is free for everyone.

Questions

Before you join

What exactly is ishéli?

An iPhone app for short, guided reflection. At your own pace: one question, a reflection in plain words, and one small thing to try. About ten minutes.

Do I pay anything now?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and needs no payment. You only start a subscription if you redeem your founding code in the App Store after launch.

When will I get access?

Soon, on iPhone first. Your code arrives by email on launch day. Android later: join and you’ll hear the day it’s ready.

What happens to what I write?

In the app, what you write is stored on your iPhone. There’s no account, and nothing you write is sent to us. If you back up your iPhone, Apple’s backup may include it. You can delete everything in the app at any time. The waitlist keeps only your email address.

What happens after the 3 free months?

Your subscription renews automatically at your founding price, €29.99 a year or €4.99 a month, until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free months end in Settings › your name › Subscriptions to avoid being charged. Your founding price stays yours while your subscription remains active.

Is this therapy?

No. ishéli is a companion for reflection, not medical or psychological care. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
