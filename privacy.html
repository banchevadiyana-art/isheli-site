Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything you write in ishéli stays on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive it, and we never see it.
  • There is no account to create. The app does not ask for your email, phone number or real name. (If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email address for our letters: see “Our website and waitlist”.)
  • The app has no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools and no data brokers. We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.
  • Our website uses Google Analytics only if you say yes in the small box at the bottom of the page. You can change your mind any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We only learn whether a subscription is active, never who you are or how you paid.
  • You can export your words or delete everything at any time, from inside the app.

Who we are

ishéli is published by Diyana Bancheva, Sofia, Bulgaria ("we", "us"). For anything about privacy, write to hello@isheli.app.

Because ishéli keeps your information on your own device, we do not collect or process the personal data you enter into the app. Where we do handle personal data — for example if you email us — we act as the data controller for that data.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

To work, ishéli stores the following on your device only:

  • The area you chose (Body, Mind or Soul), the intentions you picked and anything you wrote in your own words.
  • Your answers in practices, the stones you have placed, your progress through chapters and seasons.
  • Moves you added to your path and the days you kept them.
  • Moments you caught with "It happened", and your decisions in "Rules I never chose".
  • An optional first name, used only for the greeting.
  • Your settings: reminder time, reading comfort, haptics, sound, app lock.
  • Simple usage counts (for example, which steps were completed). They stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that store or receive your content.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not use your words to train artificial intelligence or for any other purpose.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or Health data.

Services provided by Apple

  • In-app purchases. Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple's handling of this data is described at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Face ID and passcode lock. If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. ishéli only receives a yes or no. We never receive or store biometric data.
  • Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. By default they never show your own words on the lock screen; you can change this in Settings.
  • Widget. The home-screen widget shares a small piece of information with the app (for example, that you tapped "It happened") through storage on your device.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup may include ishéli's data, protected by Apple's backup security. You control backups in your iPhone settings.

Your words may be personal

What you write may touch on your health, feelings, relationships or beliefs. That is exactly why it stays on your device. We cannot read it, and we cannot hand it to anyone.

Export and delete

  • Export: Profile › Privacy & Face ID › Export my words creates a file that you choose where to send.
  • Delete: Profile › Privacy & Face ID › Delete everything permanently erases all ishéli data on the device. Deleting the app does the same.

Because we never have a copy, we cannot restore deleted data.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep support emails for up to 24 months and then delete them. We do not use them for marketing.

Our website and waitlist

Our website, isheli.app, has no advertising. Its fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third parties.

Analytics, only with your permission

If you choose “Yes” in the small box at the bottom of the page, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited) to count visits and understand which parts of the page are useful: for example how many people try the practice demo, which topic they pick, and how many join the waitlist. Google Analytics sets cookies (names starting with _ga) that last up to 14 months, and receives your IP address, which it does not log or store for EU users. We turned off Google signals and ad personalisation, and we keep this data for 14 months. We never send your email address or anything you write to Google.

If you choose “No”, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookies are set. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules on cookies). We remember your choice on your device for six months, without a cookie. You can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer; saying no later deletes the analytics cookies from this site.

Google may process this data in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you join the waitlist, we keep:

  • your email address;
  • the topic you picked on the page, if you picked one (for example “I keep putting myself last”), so our letters can be relevant to you;
  • the date you joined and that you joined from the website.

We use this only to send you the launch email with your founding codes and, after that, about one letter a month. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to hello@isheli.app; we then stop emailing you and delete your details on request.

Emails are sent through Loops (Loops, Inc.), which stores the list on our behalf as a data processor under a data processing agreement. Our website host processes technical data such as IP addresses briefly to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. We never sell or share your email address with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. For data inside the app, you can exercise these rights yourself with the export and delete tools. For anything else, write to hello@isheli.app.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg). You can also contact the authority in the country where you live.

Age

ishéli is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly receive data from children.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS data protection and, if you turn it on, by Face ID or passcode lock inside the app. Keep your device passcode private; anyone who can unlock your phone and the app can read what you wrote.

International transfers

We do not transfer your app data anywhere. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's own terms. If you join the waitlist, Loops may process your email address in the United States; where it does, the transfer is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. If you accept website analytics, Google may process that data in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you inside the app before they apply.
